STRATOS — A maintenance mission beyond the clouds


You are a member of IKARO's Maintenance Crew, sent to cross the broken route and reach the portal waiting on the far side. Your suit was built for difficult work, not heroic adventures: reinforced white-and-grey plating, orange safety markings, a sealed black visor and a life-support pack designed to keep you alive when the ground is nowhere near.

The assignment sounds simple. Reach the other portal. Find out what remains of the route. Make it through.

But the islands have drifted apart, and the old bridges can no longer carry you across the gaps. Every leap is a question of distance. Every landing is a small victory. The sky between the islands is beautiful, quiet—and unforgiving.
The creatures that ride the wind
You are not completely alone. Across the cloud sea, strange winged creatures known as Sky Skimmers glide between the islands. They follow the currents as if the wind itself were guiding them.

Your maintenance gear has one unexpected use: its grappling line can catch the harness points carried by these creatures. Hold on, and their flight becomes your path through the open sky. Build momentum as you swing, read the arc of your movement, and release at just the right moment to reach the next island.

The Sky Skimmers are not machines, and they do not follow your commands. You must learn their rhythm, trust the movement of the wind and make each connection count.
A path made of momentum
The journey begins gently. The first islands offer room to breathe, a chance to learn how the rope catches and how a swing gathers speed. Soon the gaps grow wider, the landings narrower and the creatures harder to reach. What first felt like a simple crossing becomes a flowing sequence of jumps, catches, swings and releases.

Scattered along the route are forgotten coins and rarer treasures—small signs that someone travelled these islands long before you. They are not the reason for the mission, but every discovery makes the silent world feel a little less empty.

And always, somewhere beyond the next stretch of cloud, the exit portal waits.
Beyond the last island
There is no road beneath your boots, only drifting stone, old architecture and the endless sky. To cross STRATOS, you must learn to move with it: to trust the creatures that inhabit it, to carry your momentum forward and to let go when the moment is right.

One portal brought you here.

The other is waiting to take you home—or somewhere you have never been.

The route is yours to cross.
