STRATOS — A maintenance mission beyond the clouds


You are a member of IKARO's Maintenance Crew, sent to cross the broken route and reach the portal waiting on the far side. Your suit was built for difficult work, not heroic adventures.

The assignment sounds simple. Reach the other portal. Find out what remains of the route. Make it through.

The winged creatures known as Sky Skimmers glide between the islands.

Your maintenance gear has one unexpected use: its grappling line can catch the harness points carried by these creatures. Hold on, and their flight becomes your path through the open sky. Build momentum as you swing, read the arc of your movement, and release at just the right moment to reach the next island.

The Sky Skimmers are not machines, and they do not follow your commands. You must learn their rhythm, trust the movement of the wind and make each connection count.

Scattered along the route are forgotten coins and rarer treasures—small signs. They are not the reason for the mission, but every discovery makes the silent world feel a little less empty.

And always, somewhere beyond the next stretch of cloud, the exit portal waits.

One portal brought you here.

The other is waiting to take you home—or somewhere you have never been.

The route is yours to cross.
